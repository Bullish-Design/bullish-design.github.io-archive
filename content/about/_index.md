Bullish Design focuses on mechanical and industrial design, with product design consulting as its main offering. This site also shares research-led overviews of products, mechanisms, and design decisions.

Design notes look at how an object works, how its parts relate, and which constraints shape its form. Each article links to its sources so readers can continue the research.

The public work pages document software projects built under Bullish Design. Roxo Flex is a configurable Hugo theme. Siteman is a site manager for Hugo. These projects record the digital tools developed alongside the product design focus.
